## Who we are

- Iron Wing Dispatching provides truck dispatching for owner-operators and
  small motor carriers.
- The company is run by a former owner-operator who spent years behind the
  wheel. The motto is "By truckers, for truckers."
- We act as the carrier's dispatcher. The carrier stays the motor carrier of
  record and stays responsible for operating the truck, hours of service,
  authority, insurance and permits.
- Service is personal: a carrier always knows who is on the other end of the
  line.
- Phone: (817) 865-3016. Email: info@iron-wing-dispatching.com. Website:
  iron-wing-dispatching.com.

## Services

- Load sourcing: we search for loads that match the carrier's preferences and
  negotiate the freight rate with the broker.
- Route optimization: we plan efficient routes to save fuel and cut downtime
  and deadhead miles.
- Driver dispatching: we send rate confirmations and dispatch instructions so
  the driver can focus on driving.
- Administrative support: carrier setup packets, bills of lading, proofs of
  delivery, rate confirmations, invoices, receipts and IFTA reports (how much
  of this is included depends on the plan).
- Factoring assistance: we submit paperwork to the carrier's factoring company
  so payment comes faster.
- Compliance assistance: we help keep documents and certifications current.
- Lumper and detention: we help the carrier claim lumper and detention fees.
- Dedicated lanes: we can help a carrier secure contracts directly with
  shippers.
- Support: business-hours support on the Essential Plan, priority 24/7 support
  on the Full-Service Plan.

## Plans and pricing

The fee is one simple percentage of the linehaul rate on each load we book for
the carrier. Fuel surcharge is not included in the fee base. There is no
contract and the carrier can cancel anytime.

- **Essential Plan — 5% of the linehaul rate.** For owner-operators who handle
  the rest themselves. Includes load sourcing and rate negotiation, route
  optimization, business-hours support, and basic administrative support
  (carrier packets, BoLs, PoDs and rate confirmations).
- **Full-Service Plan — 10% of the linehaul rate.** We run the back office.
  Includes everything in Essential, plus priority 24/7 support, a dedicated
  dispatcher who knows the carrier's lanes, trailer and goals, full
  administrative support (also invoices, receipts and IFTA reports), factoring
  assistance, lumper and detention claims, and compliance assistance.
- **Fleets of 3 or more trucks:** fleet pricing is available. The dispatcher
  puts it together on the callback; no fleet rate is quoted on this call.
- If a carrier cancels, fees are still due on loads that were booked before
  they cancelled.
- Published pricing can change for future loads; a change never affects loads
  already booked.

## Common questions and objections

- **What does the percentage apply to?** The linehaul rate on each load we
  book. Fuel surcharge is not included.
- **Is there a contract?** No. There is no long-term contract and the carrier
  can stop anytime by phone or email.
- **Do you guarantee how much I will make?** No. Freight markets change
  constantly. We work to find profitable loads, but we do not guarantee any
  level of revenue, load volume or rates.
- **Do I have to take every load you find?** The carrier reviews the rate
  confirmation and dispatch instructions before accepting a load.
- **Which trailers do you dispatch?** Dry van, flatbed, reefer and car hauler.
- **Do you work with fleets?** Yes. For 3 or more trucks the dispatcher puts
  together fleet pricing.
- **What happens after I sign up?** A dispatcher reaches out by phone or email
  to go over equipment, lanes and goals.
- **Do you handle factoring?** On the Full-Service Plan we submit the
  paperwork to the carrier's factoring company. We are not a factoring
  company.
- **Is support 24/7?** Priority 24/7 support is part of the Full-Service Plan.
  The Essential Plan has business-hours support.
- **Why choose Iron Wing?** It is run by a former owner-operator who
  understands the long hours, tight delivery windows and deadhead miles; the
  service is personal; and there is no contract.
- **Will you text me?** Only with the carrier's agreement, which a dispatcher
  asks for on a call. The assistant does not sign anyone up for texts.
